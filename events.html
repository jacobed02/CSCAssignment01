Advice for Visitors

Includes: Parking , Saftey, Weather, and Accessability

Parking Information

  • Parking is available on-site and in nearby lots.
  • Follow the signs for designated parking areas.
  • Arrive early to secure a spot, especially on weekends.
  • Consider carpooling or using public transportation to reduce traffic congestion.

Safety Guidelines

  • Follow all posted safety signs and instructions from staff.
  • Keep an eye on your belongings and report any suspicious activity.
  • Stay hydrated and take breaks in shaded areas during hot weather.
  • In case of emergency, locate the nearest first aid station or contact event staff.

Weather Considerations

  • Check the weather forecast before attending the fair.
  • Bring sunscreen, hats, and sunglasses for sunny days.
  • Carry a light raincoat or umbrella in case of rain.
  • Be prepared for temperature changes and dress accordingly.

Accessibility Information

  • The fairgrounds are wheelchair accessible, with ramps and designated pathways.
  • Accessible restrooms are available throughout the venue.
  • Service animals are welcome in accordance with ADA guidelines.
  • For any specific accessibility needs, please contact event staff for assistance.
